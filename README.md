## Hi, I'm William

I'm a research scientist at [Sakana AI](https://sakana.ai) in Tokyo, where I work on new model architectures and on making frontier LLMs decode faster.

Before Sakana I spent two years at Huawei on efficient inference: speculative decoding, KV-cache quantization, and serving long contexts without running out of memory or patience. Before that I did a PhD at the University of Edinburgh on learning from noisy labels, which mostly meant asking what a model should do when its training data is wrong. Along the way I also got interested in time series forecasting and wrote a few papers on that too.

### Things I've built

- [DecodeAtlas](https://github.com/williamtoner/DecodeAtlas) — a picture guide to how frontier LLMs are actually deployed for decoding.
- [Kotoba](https://github.com/williamtoner/Kotoba) — photograph a page of a Japanese textbook and get flash cards for the vocabulary on it.
- [Kanjr](https://github.com/williamtoner/Kanjr) — an app for learning kanji.
- [SnakeBox](https://github.com/williamtoner/SnakeBox) — a search for new snake-in-the-box records, i.e. the longest induced paths in a hypercube.

Outside of work I'm usually learning Japanese (which is where Kotoba and Kanjr came from) or losing an evening to a computational puzzle (which is where SnakeBox came from).
