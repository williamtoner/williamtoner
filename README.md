## :wave: About me

I'm a **Research Scientist at [Sakana AI](https://sakana.ai)** in Tokyo, working where machine learning research meets systems: making frontier LLMs decode faster and serve long contexts efficiently. Before Sakana I spent two years at **Huawei** working on efficient LLM inference, after a **PhD at the University of Edinburgh** on robust learning from noisy labels. I've also published on time series forecasting and time series foundation models.

- :zap: **Efficient inference:** speculative decoding, KV-cache quantization and transfer, long-context serving
- :chart_with_upwards_trend: **Time series:** linear forecasting models, zero-shot foundation models, online adaptation
- :shield: **Robust learning:** label noise, robust loss functions, early stopping under noisy labels
- :jigsaw: **For fun:** computational puzzles and learning Japanese
