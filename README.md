## About

I'm a Research Scientist at Sakana AI in Tokyo, where I work on new model architectures and on making frontier LLMs decode faster.

Before Sakana I spent two years at **Huawei** working on efficient LLM inference, after a **PhD at the University of Edinburgh** on robust learning from noisy labels. I've also published on time series forecasting and time series foundation models.

- **LLM architectures and inference:** new architectures, speculative decoding, KV-cache quantization and transfer, long-context serving
- **Time series:** linear forecasting models, zero-shot foundation models, online adaptation
- **Robust learning:** label noise, robust loss functions, early stopping under noisy labels
- **Outside research:** computational puzzles and learning Japanese
